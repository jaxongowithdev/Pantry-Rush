### Full Description

Tiny Chef is a casual cooking simulation where you manage a small kitchen, prepare dishes, fulfill customer orders, and upgrade your equipment. Collect recipes, earn coins, expand your menu, and master the art of fast-paced cooking. Features multiple themes, order management, kitchen upgrades, and a collection system. Easy to learn and satisfying to play for cooking game fans.
